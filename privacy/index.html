Privacy

Short version: we collect nothing.

  • Your biodata (text and photo) is created entirely inside your own browser. It is never sent to any server.
  • To let you come back and edit, your details are saved in your browser's local storage on this device only. Tap “Clear all” in the maker to delete them, or clear your browser data.
  • There are no accounts, no cookies for tracking, no analytics and no advertising scripts. All fonts and code are served from this website itself; no third-party services are contacted.
  • When you tap “Share on WhatsApp”, your phone's own share menu or WhatsApp is opened by you; ShubhBiodata does not see what you send.
  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP address) as described in GitHub's privacy statement.
